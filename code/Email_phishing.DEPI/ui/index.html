Anti-Phishing Security Tips

            
    
                
  • Verify the Sender: Always check the full email address of the sender, not just the display name. Look for subtle spelling errors in the domain name.
    • 
                
  • Don't Click Links: Never click on links or open attachments from unexpected or suspicious emails. Hover your mouse over links to see the real destination in the status bar.
    • 
                
  • Sense of Urgency: Phishing messages often create a false sense of urgency ("Your account will be suspended now!") to rush you into making a mistake.
    • 
                
  • Personal Information: Never enter your password, credit card number, or personal details in response to an email.
